A Pomodoro timer built into Anki, plus focus tools that keep you honest while you review. Pomodoro timer
  • Focus, short break and long break, with lengths you choose.
  • Starts automatically when you begin studying a deck (optional).
  • A thin progress bar along the bottom of Anki. Click it to open the timer.
  • Open the timer from Tools > Pomodoro Timer or with Ctrl+Shift+P.
Focus options
  • Auto-reveal: shows the answer after a set number of seconds.
  • Auto-answer: once the answer is showing, marks the card Again (or buries it) after a set time.
  • Tip of my tongue button: almost have it? Adds a few seconds before the answer is revealed.
  • Studying button: adds time to look at the answer before the card is auto-answered.
  • Hotkey (default H) for whichever of those two buttons is showing.
  • Lock me into the deck: blocks going back to the deck list, the deck overview or switching decks until the deck's due cards are finished.
  • Auto-reveal and auto-answer pause during breaks and while the timer is paused.
  • While paused, the review screen shows Resume and Continue without Pomodoro (keeps the timer paused but turns auto-reveal / auto-answer back on).
Languages English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語 and 한국어. The add-on follows Anki's language automatically, or you can pick one in the timer's settings. How to use
  1. Open the timer with Ctrl+Shift+P.
  2. Under Focus Options, turn on auto-reveal / auto-answer and set the times.
  3. Study a deck. The timer starts, and the Tip of my tongue / Studying button appears next to More at the bottom of the review screen, with a countdown.
Requires Anki 2.1.50 or newer. Bugs, questions and suggestions: please open an issue on GitHub. Reviews on AnkiWeb can't be replied to, so GitHub is the best way to get a problem fixed.
